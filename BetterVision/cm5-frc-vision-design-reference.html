Sits at raw input (before Q1) so it must survive reverse battery; 16 V is above the 15 V OVP so it only catches fast transients; clamps << eFuse 60 V rating. SMB (600 W) adequate; SMC (1500 W) for margin.
MODEFault response→ GND (auto-retry)GND = auto-retry, open = latch-off. Auto-retry so a transient trip self-recovers mid-match (no power-cycle needed).
SHDNEnable (R4/R5 + Zener)R4 100 kΩ (IN_SYS→SHDN), R5 33 kΩ (SHDN→GND)~3.35 V at 13.5 V; ~1.74 V at 7 V sag (above ~1 V enable thr — confirm). Zener BZT52C4V7 (C173408) in parallel with R5 — both legs across SHDN→GND; cathode→SHDN, anode→GND (NOT in series, NOT both ends to GND). Clamps SHDN to ~4.7 V on a transient (else the ~26 V TVS clamp pushes SHDN to ~6.4 V, over its 5.5 V abs-max). Can't enable from a CM5 GPIO (CM5 powered from this eFuse). Optional CM5 GPIO pull-low-only = SW shutdown.
PGTHPGOOD threshold (R6/R7)R6 31.6 kΩ (OUT→PGTH), R7 10 kΩ (PGTH→GND)V(PGTHR) = 1.2 V (confirmed) → threshold ≈ 5.0 V, below the 7 V sag floor so PGOOD stays high through sag (TI confirms PGSET < VIN(min) is valid). Enables fast brownout-recovery + sets PGOOD threshold. Don't tie to GND (disables it).
PGOODPower-good (open-drain)→ buck-boost EN (w/ pull-up)High when FET enhanced → gate the converter so it starts only on valid input. Needs a pull-up.
FLT → GPIO16 (29)Fault flag (open-drain)→ CM5 GPIO16, 10 kΩ pull-up to 3.3 VActive-low on UV/OV/overload/PLIM/ILIM-short/thermal. Slow status flag (no speed constraint), so 10 kΩ is the relaxed sweet spot: ~0.33 mA sink when low (« 10 mA limit), clean high. Pulled to 3.3 V = clean digital flag → log faults. (GPIO16 free; don't double-book with FSTROBE-capture.)

Three things easy to miss: (1) GPIO_VREF (78) absolutely cannot float — tie to 3.3 V or the whole GPIO bank misbehaves. (2) With no SD slot, the eMMC is only flashable via nRPIBOOT + USB, so the USB-C path and the nRPIBOOT jumper are mandatory, not convenience features. (3) The TPS630701 buck-boost tops out at 16 V input — thin margin on a transient-heavy FRC rail — so the 12 V protection must use the adjustable-cutoff eFuse (TPS26631), not a passive TVS or the fixed-clamp -636.
